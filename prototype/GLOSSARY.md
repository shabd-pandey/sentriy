# Customer app glossary

Vocabulary specific to the customer-facing app in this directory. Terms the whole platform shares — *Catalog title*, *Screening*, *Watch progress*, *Booking hold* — are defined once in the [shared glossary](../GLOSSARY.md); this file does not repeat them.

## Terms

- **Title catalog**: The customer surface that lists catalog titles in *Watch order*, showing each as *Active* or *Upcoming*.
- **Customer dashboard**: The customer surface that shows a signed-in account its watch progress and bookings.
- **Suggested watch order**: The `suggested_watch_order` field in the one-time import JSON. It seeds the shared *Watch order* at import time and has no meaning afterwards.
