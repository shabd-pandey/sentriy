# 08: Managing and displaying promotional banners

**What to build:** The owner can upload and publish promotional banners independently of film posters, with optional publication dates and destinations; customers see only banners currently eligible for display.

**Blocked by:** 01: Creating the runnable platform foundation and role-safe sign-in.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The owner can upload banner artwork and create, edit, draft, publish, and unpublish banner records.
- [ ] A banner can have optional start/end publication times and an optional destination linked to a film or screening.
- [ ] Customers see only published banners within their optional publication window.
- [ ] Banner artwork is stored independently from film poster records and can be updated without changing a film's poster.
- [ ] Invalid date windows and unsupported media are rejected with visible admin feedback.
- [ ] End-to-end tests verify role authorization, upload/display, draft exclusion, and start/end scheduling behavior.
